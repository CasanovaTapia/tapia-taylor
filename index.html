FAQ

We're still working out the final details. More info will land here when the formal invitations go out.

Do I need to RSVP now?

Not yet! The formal invitation with the full schedule and RSVP follows closer to the day.

How do I get there?

TPA (Tampa International) is 35 minutes away with flights from every major airline. PIE (St. Pete–Clearwater) is a little closer and can be significantly cheaper if you're flying on Allegiant.

How do I get around?

Rideshare is easy and everywhere. If you'd like to explore Clearwater Beach and the surrounding coastline, we recommend renting a car.

What's the weather like in March?

Sunny in the upper 70s during the day and breezy evenings, perfect for celebrating outdoors!

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is our gift, but if you insist, we'll have a registry up soon.

Where should we stay?

Downtown Dunedin offers charming B&Bs just minutes from the Fenway, Clearwater has the widest range of hotels, Clearwater Beach puts you right on the water. We also have a room block at the Fenway Hotel for your convenience.
